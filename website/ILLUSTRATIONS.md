# Website illustrations

Six text-free illustrations generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Sources: `src/assets/illustrations/`. Astro produces responsive WebP derivatives; the component preserves the full composition. These are editorial illustrations, not screenshots, measured graphs or representations of a specific manufacturer's equipment.

Alternative descriptions are localized in `src/lib/illustrations.ts`, including social previews. The current workspace screenshot is retained separately in `../assets/readme/workspace.png` for the project README, outside the website.

## Shared generation prompt

Editorial illustration for MixtapeForge website. Premium minimalist digital illustration with softly beveled geometric objects, subtle 2.5D/isometric perspective, clean large readable silhouettes, restrained material shading. Deep navy #101322 seamless background, lighter navy #1a1f31 object bodies, lavender #c4adff primary objects, cyan #67dde6 small highlights, off-white #f3f2fa details. Small controlled soft shadows, subtle muted ambient light, NOT neon cyberpunk and NOT a photoreal product photograph. No paper texture, no crosshatching, no vintage manual, no beige or sepia, no people, no intricate technical diagrams, no graph axes. Square composition with important subject entirely within central 75% and ample navy margin, coherent tidy modern audio desktop aesthetic. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT anywhere: no letters, words, digits, logos, watermarks, labels, captions, pseudo-writing, UI text, ruler markings or decorative typography. Cassette labels must be completely blank or abstract solid colour blocks, meters may have a few unlabelled bars only. One illustration, not a montage or panels.
