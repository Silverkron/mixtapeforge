## Export and print

1. Open **Cover**, finish the title and track order, and inspect long names. Built-in layouts allow 15 tracks total; for more, use [a separate tracklist layout](../long-tracklists/).
2. Choose **J-card printing**, or export the A4 PDF and open it in a PDF viewer. PDF export preserves letter shapes as vector outlines; it is not a screenshot.
3. Select A4, portrait, **Actual size / 100%**. Disable fit/shrink, browser headers and footers. Confirm the preview does not clip the artwork. [Adobe documents the difference between Actual size and Fit](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/print-documents/set-up-and-print-pdfs/page-size.html).
4. Print one copy. Measure the 50 mm reference when printing directly from the app; on an exported file, measure a known card dimension instead. MixtapeForge’s flat card is 104.8 × 101.6 mm.
5. Cut the outer edge and fold along the panel boundaries, using a ruler to keep folds straight. Fit it into the case without forcing the hinge.

## Accept or correct the proof

Read the smallest title through the closed case. Check that the spine sits flat and the paper does not bow. A uniformly smaller card indicates [a scaling problem](../j-card-wrong-size/); correct the print settings before enlarging the artwork. Correct dimensions but poor fit require checking [the case and fold positions](../j-card-dimensions/).

**Ready for final paper:** measured size correct, complete tracklist legible, folds aligned, case closes freely. Keep the project and the successful print settings.

For a different template or free placement of text and images, [compare dedicated J-card tools and layout editors](../j-card-makers/) before rebuilding the file.
