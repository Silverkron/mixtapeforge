## Make the orientation test first

You need a two-page layout, paper approved for printing on both sides, a ruler and the case. **MixtapeForge currently exports one cover face**, not an imposed duplex document. Export editable SVG and build the reverse in Inkscape or another layout editor using the same panel boundaries. Supplier templates may provide separate front/back guides.

1. Make a plain-paper dummy. Mark the outside front, top edge and inside reading direction in pencil. Fold it as it will sit in the case.
2. Set up two pages with matching physical dimensions and placement. Add a small top-direction mark outside the cut area on the test only.
3. Print one two-sided proof at 100%. Choose long-edge or short-edge flipping according to your page orientation and printer; do not assume one setting works for every layout. For manual refeeding, follow the printer’s feed instructions.
4. Hold the sheet to light to compare panel boundaries. Cut and fold, then read both sides. Correct back-page rotation or position before printing again.

## Allow for real printer tolerances

A narrow border across the fold makes a small registration error obvious. Keep important text away from folds and trim, and avoid front/back alignment that requires perfect coincidence. If show-through distracts from the text, test [a more opaque paper](../j-card-paper/).

**Approve:** both faces upright when unfolded, scale unchanged, useful fold clearance and no hidden text. Finish using [the cutting and fit checks](../print-j-card/).
