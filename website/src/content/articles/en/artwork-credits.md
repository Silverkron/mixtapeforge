## Keep a small rights record

For each photo, illustration or font, save the creator, original source URL, licence or written permission, download date and any conditions. Read the actual terms: Creative Commons licences differ on commercial use, modifications and sharing adapted work. Public availability does not mean public domain.

1. Decide the intended use: one personal insert, a public download or a release for sale. Check that the permission covers that use rather than assuming all three are equivalent.
2. Prefer your own artwork or material with clear terms. If attribution is required, collect the supplied title, author, source and licence link, plus required notices.
3. Record changes such as cropping or recolouring where required. A credit should not imply that the original artist endorses your release.
4. Put a readable short credit on the insert and, when appropriate under the licence, link to a page containing the full attribution. Keep that page accessible; a tiny unreadable URL does not help the listener.

## An illustrative credit

A hypothetical layout might say: “Photo: Alex Example, ‘Night platform’, CC BY 4.0; cropped. Source and licence: …”. Replace every placeholder with the real information and check the actual licence conditions. This example grants no rights to any image.

MixtapeForge’s title/subtitle fields are limited; use [a separate layout](../long-tracklists/) for substantial credits instead of shrinking them. Finish with [a physical readability proof](../cover-typography/).

**Before sharing:** permission documented, terms followed, credit readable, modifications acknowledged where required. Unclear permission is a reason to choose another image.
