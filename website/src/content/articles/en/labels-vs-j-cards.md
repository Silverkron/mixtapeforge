## Choose what to put where

Put the title, side A/B and playback NR on the shell label. Put the full tracklist, dedication and longer credits on the [J-card](../print-j-card/). An O-card is a separate wraparound sleeve, not a sticker; see [packaging formats](../release-packaging/).

MixtapeForge makes J-cards, not adhesive-label sheets or direct shell printing. For labels, use a template supplied for the exact shell or die-cut label product. A similarly shaped online image is not a dimensional template.

## Apply one trial label

1. Check the label material is suitable for your printer and cassette shell. Print a paper outline first to test its shape.
2. With the cassette out of the deck, check clearance around hubs, openings and seams. Do not cover type-detection or record-protection holes.
3. Apply one label flat within the shell’s intended recess. Avoid thick layers and loose corners. Follow the adhesive supplier’s instructions.
4. Inspect before inserting it into the deck. If an edge lifts, do not test whether the mechanism will flatten it; replace the label with a suitable one.

Paper stickers, pad printing and direct UV printing use different templates and production rules; Duplication.com explicitly separates them. For a few personal tapes, a clear insert and a small suitable shell label are easier to control than an improvised full-shell wrap.

**Check:** side identifiable outside the case, openings unobstructed, label fully attached, insert and tape agree.
