A cover worth keeping.

Finish the tape with a readable tracklist, your own colours and a J-card you can actually print.

Get the app ↗Getting started →
01

Nine styles. Your colours.

Choose from classic studio layouts, rock, pop, synthwave, poster, home-recorded and monochrome designs. Suggested palettes help you begin; four custom colours let you make it yours.

02

Give the songs room.

The tracklist follows your two sides. Add a title and recording notes, then choose among six bundled fonts. The current templates accept up to 15 tracks in total across both sides; printing and export are disabled above that limit. Check long titles in the preview because they may be shortened.

03

Print it. Cut it. Fold it.

Use the system print dialog or export PDF and SVG. Choose A4, print at 100%, turn off fit-to-page and measure the 50 mm reference on a direct app print. For exported files, check the card’s 104.8 × 101.6 mm dimensions before cutting. Printer margins and the case itself still deserve a test print.

04

Made for home printers, too.

Monochrome designs and grayscale output help when a colour printer is not available. The preview and exports share the artwork; SVG keeps editable text with embedded fonts.
