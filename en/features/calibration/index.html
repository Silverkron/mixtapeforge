Get to know your deck and tape.

One guided recording and capture helps you understand the complete signal path and save a useful recording profile.

Get the app ↗Getting started →
01

Record the test.

Choose the output and supported sample rate. With noise reduction off, record the roughly 108-second sequence onto the tape you want to use. The countdown helps you coordinate the start; the tape must be beyond its leader.

02

Capture the playback.

Choose the input connected to the deck’s LINE OUT, check the incoming levels and capture the recorded sequence, or import a suitable stereo WAV. Progress, input meters and spectrum keep the process visible.

03

Read the results and save a profile.

Inspect channel response, noise, balance, crosstalk, level compression and clipping indicators. Save the profile with a prefilled name; deck and tape notes are optional. Conservative corrections are available for suitable results and remain a choice.

04

A profile has a context.

Keep the deck, tape, connections and controls consistent when reusing a profile. A round-trip measurement includes recording and playback errors. It cannot independently align playback, repair azimuth or transport faults, or certify wow and flutter, MOL or Dolby compatibility.
