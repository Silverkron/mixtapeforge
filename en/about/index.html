Built for people who make tapes.

A focused, local workbench for turning a collection of songs into a cassette you want to listen to.

01

Why this project exists

Preparing a tape often means juggling a player, an audio editor and a separate cover tool. MixtapeForge brings side planning, recording preparation, measurement profiles and J-cards into one desktop project.

02

Open source, local by design

The original application code is GPL-3.0-only. Audio processing runs on the computer. Plugins are optional external components. Third-party libraries, fonts and the separate Type B utility retain their own licenses.

03

How we write the guides

Articles combine practical workflows, manufacturer documentation and questions from cassette communities. Illustrations are illustrative, not measurement evidence. Technical claims must be read with the deck’s manual and the stated limits; no universal level or correction fits every machine.

04

Editorial status

The guide collection is an initial multilingual edition, assisted by generative tools. It has not undergone an independent laboratory or native-language review. No listening test or hardware result is claimed unless an article explicitly documents it. Corrections and reproducible reports are welcome through the repository.
