What to Write on a Cassette J-Card: Songs, Credits and Playback Notes

Use cassette notes to answer two questions: what is playing and how was it recorded. Keep listener details on the insert and technical notes with the project.

MixtapeForge editorial teamUpdated November 1, 20262 min read
In this guide
  1. What belongs on the cassette
  2. Keep a recording log beside the project
← Back to articles
Quick answer

Useful cassette notes answer two different questions: “What am I listening to?” and “How did I record this?” Keep the listener’s information on the insert and the technical detail with the project.

What belongs on the cassette

Write the title, artist/track order for A and B, recording date if meaningful, and playback NR such as “Dolby B” or “NR off”. Use the setting actually used, not a label chosen because it looks vintage. If you are uncertain, resolve it before printing; matching NR matters at playback.

A dedication can be one sentence. Optional stories, source editions and lengthy credits can go on another sheet rather than competing with the songs. See legible cover typography.

Keep a recording log beside the project

After the short test succeeds, write down:

  • Deck model, cassette brand/model/type and which side was recorded.
  • Audio output device, physical output pair and volume settings; REC LEVEL position.
  • Software processing/profile and whether NR encoding happened in software or in the deck.
  • Any observed problem and the exact point where it occurred.

A knob position is a repeatability note, not a calibrated voltage. If you move a cable or change output volume, update the log and repeat the level check.

Illustrative note: “A/B recorded on 26 October; Type I; hardware Dolby B; output and REC LEVEL unchanged after test; check B’s final fade.” Replace this with your actual conditions.

MixtapeForge saves project settings and exports its tracklist from Cover. Keep a separate plain-text note for hardware positions and observations; do not assume the project knows them. This makes later copies easier to reproduce without turning the insert into a service sheet.
