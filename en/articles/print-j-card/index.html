J-cards & printing

How to Make and Print a Cassette J-Card at the Correct Size

Print one correctly sized paper proof before using your final stock.

MixtapeForge editorial teamUpdated November 1, 20262 min read
In this guide
  1. Export and print
  2. Accept or correct the proof
← Back to articles
Quick answer

Print one correctly sized paper proof before using your final stock. You need MixtapeForge, a printer, ordinary A4 paper, a millimetre ruler, scissors or a cutter with a mat, and the actual cassette case.

Export and print

  1. Open Cover, finish the title and track order, and inspect long names. Built-in layouts allow 15 tracks total; for more, use a separate tracklist layout.
  2. Choose J-card printing, or export the A4 PDF and open it in a PDF viewer. PDF export preserves letter shapes as vector outlines; it is not a screenshot.
  3. Select A4, portrait, Actual size / 100%. Disable fit/shrink, browser headers and footers. Confirm the preview does not clip the artwork. Adobe documents the difference between Actual size and Fit.
  4. Print one copy. Measure the 50 mm reference when printing directly from the app; on an exported file, measure a known card dimension instead. MixtapeForge’s flat card is 104.8 × 101.6 mm.
  5. Cut the outer edge and fold along the panel boundaries, using a ruler to keep folds straight. Fit it into the case without forcing the hinge.

Accept or correct the proof

Read the smallest title through the closed case. Check that the spine sits flat and the paper does not bow. A uniformly smaller card indicates a scaling problem; correct the print settings before enlarging the artwork. Correct dimensions but poor fit require checking the case and fold positions.

Ready for final paper: measured size correct, complete tracklist legible, folds aligned, case closes freely. Keep the project and the successful print settings.

For a different template or free placement of text and images, compare dedicated J-card tools and layout editors before rebuilding the file.
