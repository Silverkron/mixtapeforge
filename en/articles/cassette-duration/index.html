How Long Is a C90? Leaders, Gaps and Planning Both Sides

A C90 is nominally 90 minutes across both sides, about 45 minutes per side.

MixtapeForge editorial teamUpdated November 1, 20262 min read
In this guide
  1. Calculate usable time
  2. If the final song is cut off
← Back to articles
Quick answer

A C90 is nominally 90 minutes across both sides, about 45 minutes per side. You cannot put a 50-minute side A on it merely because side B is shorter. Plan each side separately, including the silences you actually want to record.

Calculate usable time

You need the intended cassette, your complete source files and MixtapeForge. Set the project’s side capacities to match the cassette, then keep a margin rather than relying on an unusually generous tape length. The app’s side panels show programme duration and remaining space; a red excess means the prepared programme exceeds capacity minus margin.

  1. Arrange A and B in the Mixtape screen. Include pauses, fades and trimmed track lengths; build the musical order first.
  2. Compare each side’s displayed duration with its available time. Move a whole track to the other side or choose a shorter version if necessary.
  3. Audition transitions after moving tracks. Removing a pause may save time but can join two songs abruptly.
  4. Test the actual cassette’s end on your deck before relying on a tight fit. The counter is not necessarily elapsed minutes; a stopwatch can help. Leave room for the initial recordable silence as well as the ending.

Illustrative calculation: a nominal 30:00 side with 0:30 reserved allows 29:30. Music totalling 28:50 plus five 0:03 gaps totals 29:05, leaving 0:25. This is a planning example, not a measured C60 capacity. Do not count a gap twice if the audio file already contains it.

If the final song is cut off

Check actual tape length, start position and pauses before changing playback speed. Speeding up music changes pitch and is not the normal solution. Use a shorter programme and repeat the side. Very long/thin tape also needs explicit support from the deck’s manual.

Ready to record: both sides fit independently; gaps included once; leader and tail allowance considered; last track checked on tape.
