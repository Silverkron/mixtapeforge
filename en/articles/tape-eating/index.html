Why Is My Cassette Deck Eating Tapes?

Stop damage first, then separate a faulty cassette from a transport that cannot take up the tape.

MixtapeForge editorial teamUpdated November 1, 20262 min read
In this guide
  1. What to do now
  2. When to test again
← Back to articles
Quick answer

If loose tape gathers around the capstan or comes out of the cassette, stop playback immediately. The transport is moving tape without reliably taking it up. Repeated PLAY/REW attempts can turn a recoverable loop into a creased or torn recording.

What to do now

You need a light and the deck’s manual. No software can stop a mechanical fault reliably or reconstruct torn magnetic tape.

  1. Press STOP once. Switch off and disconnect power.
  2. Open the door only if it releases normally. If the cassette remains trapped, do not pull the shell or exposed tape and do not force EJECT. Seek service; the mechanism may still grip the tape.
  3. If the cassette comes out freely, inspect the exposed tape without touching its magnetic surface. Creases, folds, loose oxide or a damaged shell are reasons not to put it back into a good machine.
  4. For an undamaged cassette with only ordinary slack, gently take up slack by turning a hub with a suitable pencil, without tensioning it tightly. This is not a remedy for tape already wrapped around the deck’s mechanism.
  5. Inspect and clean the accessible path according to the model manual. Cracked or sticky rollers and absent take-up need mechanical attention.

When to test again

Only after the mechanism is free and the tape path is dry, use a disposable known-good cassette. Watch it briefly and stop at the first abnormal movement. Do not use an irreplaceable recording as the next test.

If multiple cassettes fail, stop using the deck and arrange repair. If one damaged cassette fails while others run normally, investigate that cassette rather than changing every deck. Following repair, inspect damaged recordings for dropouts and transfer valuable material before further handling. A software EQ or recording profile cannot cure this fault.
