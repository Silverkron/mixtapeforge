Designing a Cassette Release: J-Card, Labels, Credits and Inserts

Pick the packaging before designing the artwork.

MixtapeForge editorial teamUpdated November 1, 20262 min read
In this guide
  1. Match format to the contents
  2. Approve one physical package
← Back to articles
Quick answer

Pick the packaging before designing the artwork. A template for a plastic case insert will not automatically fit a card sleeve, and a beautiful file cannot correct the wrong folds.

Match format to the contents

J-card in a plastic case: front, spine and short flap; a straightforward choice for a tracklist and short credits. Extra-panel J-card: additional folds provide room for lyrics or notes, but increase thickness. U-card: a longer rear panel can interfere with hub-lock posts unless the case or die-cut accommodates them. O-card: a sleeve wrapping the cassette, usually open at the ends, rather than an insert for the same case.

These are functional distinctions. Suppliers name panel counts differently, so inspect the actual template rather than ordering by “three panels” alone. Check dimensions and panel boundaries.

Approve one physical package

  1. Get the case or sleeve you intend to use, along with its current supplier template. Confirm front/back orientation, folds, trim and required bleed.
  2. Make an unprinted paper dummy. Insert the cassette and close or slide it as intended. Ensure no flap catches on posts and nothing needs force.
  3. Place title, artist, A/B identification and useful recording notes where they remain visible. Put optional long text on extra panels.
  4. Print and fold a complete proof on the intended stock. Check spine readability, paper thickness and access to the cassette before ordering quantities.

MixtapeForge currently produces one fixed J-card geometry. Export editable SVG for a different layout, or use the supplier’s template in a separate editor; the app does not generate O-cards or automatic fold-out panels.

Ready: correct physical format, readable identifiers, no forced closure, finished proof retained. Pair it with the audio checks for a small release.

Keep shell identification separate from the insert: labels and J-cards need different templates.
