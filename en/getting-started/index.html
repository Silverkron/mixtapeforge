Your first tape, from beginning to end.

A practical first session: prepare one side, check the sound, record and make the cover.

01

Connect the deck

Connect the computer or audio interface output to LINE IN on the cassette deck. For measurement capture, connect LINE OUT to a suitable line input. Start at a moderate listening level and select the intended output in the app.

How to Record a Cassette Tape from a Computer →

02

Build the two sides

Import local audio, choose the cassette length and arrange side A and side B. Leave space for pauses and the non-recordable leader. Listen to the transitions before recording.

How to Make a Cassette Mixtape: Planning Side A and Side B →

03

Make a short check

Use a loud passage to set the deck’s REC LEVEL without audible overload. Start with processing off; apply normalization or EQ for a reason. If the deck handles Dolby B encoding, do not also encode the same recording in software.

Cassette Recording Levels: Avoid Distortion Without Recording Too Quietly →

04

Record and listen back

Move beyond the leader, press REC on the deck and start the side in the app. Keep output and record controls fixed. Record side B, then listen back on the intended player with the appropriate noise-reduction setting.

Dolby B, C or S: Which Playback Setting Should You Use? →

05

Print the J-card

Open the cover workspace, add a title and notes, choose a style and check the tracklist. Print at 100% and confirm the reference size before cutting and folding.

How to Make and Print a Cassette J-Card at the Correct Size →
